Privacy policy

Updated November 15, 2026

In short: what you keep in Novelo stays on your phone. The app has no account, no ads and no tracking. To hear, see and organize, what you say and the photos you send pass through Novelo's server to an artificial intelligence service, and the answer comes back: Novelo's server keeps neither what you said nor the answer.

Who looks after your data

Novelo is made by Jose Miguel Villamediana Osorio, an independent developer in Brazil, who is responsible for the processing of data described here under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD). Contact: miguel.villamediana@outlook.com.

What stays on your phone

Everything you keep (tasks, notes, diary, photos and the audio the app holds on to) stays in the app's storage on your phone. Novelo asks for no account and no login.

Novelo's intelligence (the default)

To turn speech into text, say what a photo shows and organize it all into tasks, notes and diary, the app sends over the internet to Novelo's server, which passes it on to an artificial intelligence model and returns the answer:

  • your voice: the recording, in pieces, to become text. On the phone, the audio is deleted once it becomes text, unless the sound is what is worth keeping (a melody you sing, for example);
  • your photos and documents: the photo (reduced) to be described; of a document, its name, the start of its text and, when it has no text, a picture of its first page;
  • the text of what you said or wrote, and what the model needs to understand the request: your open tasks, the day's diary, the last items kept and those that may have to do with the subject;
  • what you type in the search, to find by meaning as well (when there is no model on the phone).

Novelo's server keeps none of it: not the speech, not the photo, not the text, not the answer. It passes the request on to providers of artificial intelligence models, today through OpenRouter, which are outside Brazil. Those providers may log and keep what they receive, under each one's policies. If you would rather none of it leave your control, use your own intelligence (below).

The text of a photo is read on the phone itself (with Google's ML Kit, which runs on the device; it may send Google technical usage and performance data, never the photo). When you pick photos from the gallery, the app gets only the ones you picked. Photos taken with the app's camera are also saved to the phone's gallery (Novelo folder).

What Novelo's server keeps

There is no login: each installation makes a key on the device itself and signs what it asks with it. Of the device, the server keeps only what it needs to work and to prevent abuse:

  • the public half of that key, the app's version, the result of checking that the device and the app are genuine, and when it was first and last seen;
  • a scrambled identifier of the device (there is no way back from it to the original), so that the trial doesn't start over on a reinstall, and the device's plan (trial or Novelo+);
  • of each request, only numbers: the time, the model that answered, how long it took, its size and its cost. Those records are deleted after 30 days; only hourly totals remain, tied to no device.

The internet address (IP) is not recorded.

Your own intelligence

Whoever prefers can use, in Settings:

  • the model on the phone, which hears and sees without internet: nothing leaves the device. It is downloaded once from Hugging Face, which, as with any download, sees the device's internet address;
  • your API (OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Azure or another): the request goes straight to the service you set up, on your account and under its policy, without passing through Novelo's server. Your key stays on the phone, encrypted by Android.

Backup

The backup is a file the app writes to the folder you choose (on the phone or in a cloud folder you chose yourself). Novelo does not send the backup anywhere else.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to record what you say, only when you press the button.
  • Camera: to take photos inside the app.
  • Notifications and alarms: reminders, and the notice that the app is listening or organizing.
  • Internet: hearing, seeing and organizing with the intelligence.

What Novelo does not do

It shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking tools, and does not sell or share your data with anyone beyond what is described here. Google Play may collect install and crash data for the app, under Google's privacy policy.

How long, and how to delete

What you keep stays on the phone until you delete it in the app or uninstall it. Uninstalling deletes everything from the device, except the backup files you saved outside the app. You can see, correct and delete each item in the app itself. On Novelo's server, the records of each request last 30 days, and the device's record is deleted on request. To exercise any right under the LGPD (including asking for your device's record to be deleted: quote the device code shown in Settings, under the version) or to ask questions, write to miguel.villamediana@outlook.com.

Children

Novelo is not made for children under 13.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the news shows up in the app's release notes.
